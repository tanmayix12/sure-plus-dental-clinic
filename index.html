Advanced Treatments & Specialities

Professional Dental Cleaning & Teeth Whitening for a Brighter Smile

Yellowish teeth, surface stains or tartar can affect the appearance of your smile. Professional dental cleaning can help remove plaque and tartar buildup, leaving your teeth feeling cleaner and your gums healthier. For suitable patients, professional teeth whitening/bleaching may also help brighten the appearance of the smile.

Custom Dental Veneers for a Natural, Balanced Smile

Veneers can help enhance the appearance of teeth and create a brighter, more balanced and natural-looking smile.

Natural. Elegant. Personalized.
Every smile is different, so veneer planning should be tailored to your facial features, dental needs and desired appearance.

Dental Implants for Missing Teeth: Single to Full-Mouth Solutions

From replacing a single missing tooth to comprehensive full-mouth rehabilitation, dental implants can be planned according to individual needs.

  • Single Tooth Implant: Replaces an individual missing tooth seamlessly without affecting neighboring teeth.
  • Multiple Implants: Restores functionality and appearance when several teeth are missing.
  • Full Mouth Implant Rehabilitation: Comprehensive restoration for complete arches to restore chewing function, speech, and appearance.

Advanced Root Canal Treatment to Help Preserve Your Natural Tooth

With modern diagnostic technology, rotary/advanced endodontic instrumentation and careful treatment planning, root canal treatment can help preserve a natural tooth when clinically appropriate. If you are experiencing severe toothache, sensitivity or swelling, a dental evaluation can help identify the cause and determine the appropriate treatment.

Professional Tooth Extraction & Wisdom Tooth Surgery

Professional and carefully planned tooth extractions, including wisdom tooth removal, with a focus on patient comfort, safety and proper aftercare.

Bleeding Gums & Pyorrhea: Why Early Diagnosis Matters

Healthy teeth need healthy gums. Bleeding, swollen or sensitive gums can be signs of gum disease and should not be ignored. With proper diagnosis and timely treatment, gum health can be assessed and managed effectively.

Smile Designing & Aesthetic Dentistry: Personalized to You

Not every beautiful smile looks the same. Your smile should complement your facial features, personality and natural appearance. Our approach to smile design focuses on creating a balanced, natural-looking result that is personalized to you.

Design your smile. Enhance your confidence.

Accidental & Fractured Jaw Surgery: Specialized Care

Specialized evaluation and surgical care for dental and jaw injuries following accidents or facial trauma, with treatment planned according to the nature and severity of the injury.

Digital Dental Impressions: A More Comfortable Way to Plan Crowns & Bridges

Modern digital scanning technology provides a more comfortable and precise alternative to traditional dental impressions. It can be used for planning and creating dental restorations such as crowns and bridges.

"Getting a dental crown? Your dental impression can now be digital."

Digital Dental X-Rays for Accurate Diagnosis

Digital dental X-rays provide quick, detailed images that help our dental team assess oral health and plan appropriate treatment with quick imaging and detailed visualization.

Intraoral Camera Technology for Complete Transparency

See your dental health more clearly. An intraoral camera provides detailed images of your teeth and gums, helping us explain findings and treatment recommendations more clearly.
